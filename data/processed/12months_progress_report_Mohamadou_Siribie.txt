   2. Progress Report of the Training 2.1 Introduction The Clinical Research and Development (CRD) Fellowship programme was jointly initiated by the World Health Organisation/ Special Program for Research and Training in Tropical Diseases (WHO - TDR) and the European and Developing Countries Clinical Trial Partnership (EDCTP), in 2014 as a continuity of WHO – TDR Career Development Fellowship, started in 1999. This fellowship programme aim at promoting high quality clinical research in low- and middle-income countries by offering a year-long training fellowship to young research scientists, so as to develop and consolidate best practices in clinical research and development within pharmaceutical companies or products development partnership.  The International Vaccine Institute (IVI) is my host Institution (www.ivi.org). It is an international non-for-profit organization established in 1997 in Seoul, Republic of Korea, by an initiative of the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP). As a Global Vaccine Research Institute, IVI is nurturing the vision of developing countries free of suffering from infectious diseases by discovering, developing and delivering safe, effective and affordable vaccine for global public health. In collaboration with international scientific communities, public health organizations, governments and industries, the activities of IVI span all the vaccine spectrum and they are currently focused on diarrheal diseases (Cholera, typhoid fever, shigella…) and dengue. Its ongoing field activities involved three continents including Asia, Latina America and Africa.  My home institution is ‘Le Groupe de Recherche Action en Santé (GRAS)’, headquartered in Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso and aims to become a hub of excellence for biomedical research. Since its creation in 2008, GRAS scientists have carried out several Clinical Trials (phase II, III/IV drugs and vaccines clinical trials), Epidemiological Studies and Health System Research for various diseases (malaria, pneumonia, hepatitis B…). Here, we report the final progress in the framework of the CRD fellowship programme, and the activities performed within 12 months internship. 2.2 Training Objectives As stated in my training plan, I was expected to increase my skills at the end of this fellowship scheme in: -­‐ Advanced clinical research methodology,
